# Privacy Policy — Blob.io Skin Selector

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Blob.io Skin Selector processes limited information locally to provide its skin-selection functionality.

## Information processed

The extension may process:

- The player's Blob.io nickname.
- Blob.io game-page and connection data needed to apply the selected skin.
- The user's selected skin preference.

## How information is used

This information is used only to display the skin selector and apply the skin chosen by the user.

## Storage

Skin preferences are stored locally in the user's browser. The developer does not collect or store this information on external servers.

## Sharing

The extension does not sell user data or transfer it to third parties for advertising, analytics, lending, or unrelated purposes. Necessary game information is sent only to Blob.io as part of normal gameplay.

## External resources

Public skin metadata and images may be retrieved from official Blob.io services using secure HTTPS connections.

## Data retention

The developer does not retain user data. Users can remove locally stored preferences by uninstalling the extension or clearing the website's browser storage.
